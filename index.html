I cannot promise that the stars will never fade, or that the seasons will forever remain the same. But I can promise you this:

When the world has moved on, when the roads have changed, and when time has taken us to places we never imagined, a part of me will still be standing where you left it.

I will not chase the hands of time or beg the seasons to bring you back. I will simply wait, not because I have nowhere else to go, but because some souls are worth a patience that even time cannot measure.

I hope you know that I haven’t forgotten a single thing about you. Not even the smallest details, the little things you probably said without thinking twice, the things you might not even remember telling me, like I still remember how you told me you love looking at the stars, as though the universe holds stories only your eyes can understand.

I remember your favourite colours, red and black. I remember how much you love BTS, how you can get lost in the pages of a good novel, and even something as simple as the fact that pawpaw isn’t your friend.

I remember the little things, too. The things you probably mentioned casually, never imagining that someone would hold on to them so dearly. Somehow, they all stayed with me.

You’re on my mind every day, in the quietest moments and sometimes in the middle of the busiest ones. Sometimes through a song, sometimes beneath a beautiful night sky, sometimes when I see a novel and wonder what story has captured your attention lately. And sometimes, for no reason at all, you simply cross my mind.

Time passes, days change, but somehow, I still find myself remembering you. And if it takes a thousand sunsets, let them come. If it takes a thousand winters, let them pass.

For I never wished to be the reason you hurried, nor the voice that made you feel trapped. I only wished to be the place your heart could return to when it finally finds its way home.

I’m not telling you any of this to manipulate you, make you feel guilty, or convince you to be with me. I don’t want affection that comes from pressure or obligation. Or mistake these words for an attempt to change your mind, or persuade you. You owe me nothing. I just felt you deserved to know how special you are in the chapter in my life.

I still feel the same way I felt for you from day one… And perhaps that’s what makes it so difficult to explain how much you mean to me…

I cannot promise what tomorrow holds, and I wouldn’t ask you to put your life on pause for me. But if life ever brings you back to my doorstep, if someday your heart finds its way towards mine, I hope you won’t find a stranger waiting.

You’ll find someone who still remembers the girl who loved the stars, adored red and black, found happiness in BTS, escaped into the worlds of novels, and knew better than to go near pawpaw.

And until then, I wish you beautiful days, peaceful nights, and a thousand reasons to smile.

Maybe I’ll always have a little corner of my heart where the thought of you remains. Not as an obligation. Not as a regret. Just as someone I once wished to have met a little sooner, and someone I still wish nothing but happiness.

…Funny how someone can read a thousand novels and still become a story that someone else never learns how to put down.

Time passes,
but my hope remains.

— yours truly…SNA
